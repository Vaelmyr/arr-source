You may use the Arr Source name without separate permission when the use is truthful and does not imply that your project, product, service, or distribution is official, endorsed by, or affiliated with the Arr Source project. Examples include:

- Referring to the official Arr Source project in documentation, articles, reviews, tutorials, or discussions.
